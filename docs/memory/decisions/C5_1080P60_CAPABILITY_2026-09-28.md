# C5 — 1080p60 as a capability: the rule's outcome

**Status:** RULE OUTCOME, 2026-09-28. Record:
`../evidence/C5_M1_1080P60_PROFILE_2026-09-28.md`.

## The capability, by stage

| stage | result | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| client decoder (onn) | **CAPABLE** — advertises 1080p60 H.264 (`performance-point-1920x1080` 60); delivered 1920x1080 buffers at ≥ 59.69 rendered fps on the adopted APK, no client change | codec XML; SurfaceFlinger; A-hold reports |
| host encoder | **CAPABLE** — 1080p60 offline 59.99 fps mean, p5 59.98; +1 % host CPU | `offline/` |
| stream over the 5 GHz hop | **NOT CAPABLE** at 15,750 kbps / cap 200,000 — all three A holds missed spikes (517-529/min) and post-FEC video loss (123-181/min); A1 and A3 the 250 ms gap | six interleaved holds, `c5_m1_score.txt` |

The loss is the per-frame burst (`P6`'s mechanism) at 2.25× the frame
size: ~150 frames/min of ≥ 80 packets, losses of 94-111 packets per event.

## What it means

- **1080p60 is capability-gated, and on this link the gate is closed at
  parity.** The adopted profile stays `native_game_720p60_reference`.
- The candidate profile and the selector stay in the tree, never the
  default, for any future arm (`TOOLS.md`).
- The source caveat stands: the RetroArch window is 879x720, so a 1080p
  stream on it is an upscale.
- Arms that could change the outcome (a lower bitrate, a tighter 1080p cap,
  a larger source window) are **the user's call**, each with its own
  pre-registered holds. Nothing is adopted.
- The night's B2 hold missed the baseline's loss (11.25/min) and gap
  (522 ms) rows; B1 and B3 met every row.

## Appended 2026-10-03 — C5-M5: the rung as the path (the user's reading of 2026-10-01)

The user's reading of 2026-10-01 reopened C5: 1080p60 is delivered
**through the adaptive ladder, as a rung above 7000**, not only as a
characterization. C5-M4A gave the stream a native 1080p source, the PS1
at 4x in a 1920×1080 window. C5-M5 built the rung.

Record: `../evidence/C5_M5_1080P_RUNG_2026-10-03.md`. Patch:
`../patches/C5-M5_1080P_RUNG.md`.

**What is built: the rung, behind a flag, off by default.**

- `PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_TOP=1080p` adds 12,600 kbps at 1920×1080 (the
  c3 arm's argv) above 7000.
- It is entered from 7000 only, after ≥ 435 clean of the last 450
  reports.
- It is left by the existing triggers; the mild bar goes to 7000 / 720p.
- Re-entry is held for 10 min; leave-entry-leave → HOLD.
- Absent, every decision is the closed controller's (0 differences over
  377 series).

**What it showed:**

- **Client capable, mid-session**: the size change needs no client
  change. That was the one unknown C5-M4 named.
- **The mechanism**: the injection session passed, with sized switches
  costing 287-403 ms against 128-225 ms bitrate-only.
- **A 2-h night from 01:06 WORKS AS A RUNG**: one entry at 111.5 min,
  then 8.5 min at 1080p with loss 2.0/min.
- **A 30-min daytime hold did not reach the entry** (431 of 450).

**What it does not change:**

- The gate reading "stream not shown at parity" stands for a **fixed**
  1080p profile.
- The rung is the path the user chose instead: 1080p when the link
  allows it, 720p when it does not.
- On this link the entry is rare. The leave rule waits for a sustained
  shortfall that the 1080p's bursty loss does not produce.

**Not adopted. Open, the user's:**

1. the picture look at 1080p on the TV, which decides whether the rung is
   worth adopting (nothing perceptual is a gate otherwise);
2. adopting the flag by default;
3. the rung's `nft` capacity night.
