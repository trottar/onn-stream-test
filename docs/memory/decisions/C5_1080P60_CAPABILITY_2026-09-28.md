# C5 — 1080p60 as a capability: the rule's outcome

**Status:** RULE OUTCOME, 2026-09-28. Record:
`../evidence/C5_M1_1080P60_PROFILE_2026-09-28.md`.

## The capability, by stage

| stage | result | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| client decoder (onn) | **CAPABLE** — advertises 1080p60 H.264 (`performance-point-1920x1080` 60); delivered 1920x1080 buffers at ≥ 59.69 rendered fps on the adopted APK, no client change | codec XML; SurfaceFlinger; A-hold reports |
| host encoder | **CAPABLE** — 1080p60 offline 59.99 fps mean, p5 59.98; +1 % host CPU | `offline/` |
| stream over the 5 GHz hop | **NOT CAPABLE** at 15,750 kbps / cap 200,000 — all three A holds missed spikes (517-529/min) and post-FEC video loss (123-181/min); A1 and A3 the 250 ms gap | six interleaved holds, `c5_m1_score.txt` |

The loss is the per-frame burst (`P6`'s mechanism) at 2.25× the frame
size: ~150 frames/min of ≥ 80 packets, losses of 94-111 packets per event.

## What it means

- **1080p60 is capability-gated, and on this link the gate is closed at
  parity.** The adopted profile stays `native_game_720p60_reference`.
- The candidate profile and the selector stay in the tree, never the
  default, for any future arm (`TOOLS.md`).
- The source caveat stands: the RetroArch window is 879x720, so a 1080p
  stream on it is an upscale.
- Arms that could change the outcome (a lower bitrate, a tighter 1080p cap,
  a larger source window) are **the user's call**, each with its own
  pre-registered holds. Nothing is adopted.
- The night's B2 hold missed the baseline's loss (11.25/min) and gap
  (522 ms) rows; B1 and B3 met every row.

**The user's decisions, 2026-10-05 ("Yep agreed"):**

1. **The 1080p rung: NOT ADOPTED.** Built and validated mechanically
   (C5-M5 / C5-M5B: no client change for the size switch; 287-649 ms per
   switch; the entry at 415 of 450; 105 min at 1080p overnight), and read
   as buying nothing visible on PS1 at the TV while costing ~1.8× the wire
   rate, the switch gaps and the onn's 1080p decode jitter (~300 spikes
   ≥ 20 ms per minute). It stays behind
   `PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_TOP=1080p`, off, kept for a source with
   1080p detail (PS2-class, Phase H).
2. **The `remaster` preset: NOT ADOPTED.** The levers stay available per
   session behind `PRIVYHUB_PS1_LOOK` for later cores.
3. **The 4x PS1 source stays adopted** (C5-M4A), unchanged.
4. **Phase C closes** on this reading.

**One correction to the reading's evidence, from the logs.** Look 3 was
not at 1080p. Its `INCREASE_1080P` injection came 6.6 s into the session,
and the 60-s session-age guard refused it; all 74 of the session's
reports were at 7000 / 1280×720. So Looks 1 and 3 showed the same
picture, and the 1080p picture has not been seen on the TV. Decision 1
still stands on its other grounds (S3b's spike row missed by the onn's
1080p decode rate, the switch gaps, the wire rate), and nothing is
adopted either way. `TOOLS.md`'s hand steps now say to inject ≥ 60 s
after PLAYING and to check 1920×1080.

**What exists, all off by default:**

- the 1080p rung (12,600 kbps, 1920×1080) behind
  `PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_TOP=1080p`, its entry ≥ 415 of 450, and since
  C5-CLOSE the rung window skips reports while recovery is not PLAYING
  (S2b's frozen-picture window cannot qualify);
- the look presets behind `PRIVYHUB_PS1_LOOK` (`remaster`, the
  `measure-*` levers) and `tools/ps1_look.sh` (its `--attract` fixed);
- the 1080p candidate profiles behind `PRIVYHUB_NATIVE_PROFILE_ID`
  (C5-M1/M2/M3);
- **adopted:** the 4x PS1 source (1920×1080 window), streamed at 720p.

**What was learned:**

- On this hop the limit on 1080p is the client and the frame-size tail,
  not the host: the onn's 1080p decode runs ~300-600 spikes ≥ 20 ms per
  minute in every 1080p hold, against < 200; the per-frame burst at the
  1080p frame size drives loss at parity (C5-M1).
- The link's loss is bursty and time-of-day: rare, large bursts at 1080p
  (no loss-count leave could catch them, C5-M5B); LINK-L1 (80 MHz) MIXED,
  LINK-L2 (40 MHz) TIME OF DAY on the boundary; 40 MHz did not help.
- A frozen picture streams clean, so a window filled during a recovery
  pause overstates the link (S2b).
- A mid-session size change needs no client change (C5-M5 R0).
- On PS1 the source's internal resolution is what shows (4x); a 1080p
  stream of it was not judged better (the look; see the correction).

**What would reopen it:** a source with 1080p detail (PS2-class, Phase H)
or a different client (a decoder without the onn's 1080p jitter); a wish
to look at the rung's real 1080p picture on the TV (`TOOLS.md`, the
1080p rung, inject ≥ 60 s after PLAYING) would be a look, not a reopening.
