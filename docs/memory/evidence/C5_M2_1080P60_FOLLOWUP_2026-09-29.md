## Outcome

**INCONCLUSIVE (link), on both screening nights.** The confirmation
night is **NOT RUN**.

**The pre-registered rule** (`c5_m2_preregistration.txt`, sha256
`e2e0a542…`, written before night 1):

- If two or more of the four adopted B holds miss the baseline, the night
  is INCONCLUSIVE (link).
- It is re-run once before anything else is decided.

**What happened:**

- Night 1: 3 of 4 B holds missed.
- The re-run: 4 of 4 missed.
- So the screening decided nothing. No confirmation candidate exists by
  rule, and night 2 did not run.
- A second re-run was not made. The task allows one, and the queue's
  two-retry limit applies.

**Why: the channel.** The B holds are the adopted profile, which met the
baseline cold and warm in D-BASE. In the T2 figures
(`c5_m2_night1_link.txt`, `c5_m2_night1_rerun_link.txt`):

- the onn's link rate was **195 Mbit/s all night** (N1's clean hold at
  16:46Z: 260), with RSSI −68 to −70 and the Opal's signal −72 to −74;
- the **Opal's tx retries per 20-minute B hold** tracked the B loss:
  - night 1: 201k, 200k, 125k, **53k**; loss 21.6, 16.0, 12.7, **1.0**/min;
  - the re-run: 166k, 196k, 235k, 161k; loss 25.0, 18.6, 47.2, 34.9/min.
- **The one B hold with ~50k retries met every row.** Within these two
  nights, the loss tracked retransmission load on an unchanged radio link.
  That points at a busy channel, not the stream. The same evening, N2's
  live hold (18:33Z) missed its loss row.
- **Correction (2026-09-30, after C5-M3's night).** The retry count is
  not a sufficient predictor across nights. C5-M3's B holds missed the
  baseline at 66-81k retries per hold, and its one clean B hold ran at 77k.
  So "quiet" cannot be scheduled by that number alone
  (`C5_M3_LOW_RUNG_SCREENING_2026-09-29.md`).

## Reported, not decided

This is what the two nights say about 1080p. They are data, not a rule
outcome.

- **No arm met the targets on either night**, and each missed by more
  than the B holds beside it did.
  - Night 1: c3 at 313 spikes / 35 loss against B's 22-36 / 1-22.
  - The re-run: c3 at 592 / 519.
  - For reference, C5-M1's parity candidate had 517-529 spikes and
    123-181 loss.
- **The 90 KB cap removes the ≥ 80-packet frames at 1080p** (c1 and c3:
  0/min, against c2's 107-184/min and C5-M1's ~150/min). **But it binds
  constantly:**
  - c1 at parity hit the cap 49-57 s of every minute; its largest frame
    sat at the cap (p50 ≥ 85.8 KB);
  - c3 hit it 13-24 s/min;
  - the adopted 720p hits it ~10 s/min.
  - A frame held at 90 KB at 2.25× the pixels is a heavily compressed
    IDR. This is the cost the user's picture check would read against.
- **c2's scaled cap (160 KB)** let the burst back: 107-184 frames/min of
  ≥ 80 packets, the most spikes of any arm.
- **The largest gaps** came on the arms (938-2029 ms on c1 and c3 in the
  re-run). The B holds' gaps (316-460 ms) show the channel took part.
- **Host:** CPU 9-11 %, GPU 7-10 %, encoder 35-37 % of a core; the host
  (Tctl) 56-59 °C, the onn 62-72 °C. No thermal flag.
