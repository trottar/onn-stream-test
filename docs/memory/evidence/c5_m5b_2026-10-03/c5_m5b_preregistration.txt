SECTION 1 -- THE ENTRY: CANDIDATES, REPLAY, SELECTION
Candidates (the rung window keeps the blend's window rules -- evaluated reports since the last SSRC
change and its blackout, resyncs enter as not clean, stale reports skipped -- and the blend's
clean: fps >= 57, queue <= 1, gap <= 150; every other gate unchanged):
  E405 E410 E415 E420 E425 E430 E435 : >= k of the last 450 (N = 450, 15 min);
  E300 : >= 280 of the last 300 (N = 300, 10 min; 93.3 %, the clean fraction of 420 of 450).
STRICTNESS ORDER (fixed here, strictest first): by the required clean fraction, and at an equal
fraction the longer window is stricter:
  E435 (96.7 %) > E430 (95.6) > E425 (94.4) > E420 (93.3, N 450) > E300 (93.3, N 300)
  > E415 (92.2) > E410 (91.1) > E405 (90.0).
Replay: every candidate with the rung flag from the session's start (the C5-M5 replay's method,
tools/c5_m5_replay.py extended), the existing leave triggers after an entry, on:
  THE TEN REAL-INPUT HOLDS (the selection set): C3-L4-N1 H, C3-L4-N2 H, C3-L4-D1 B3b H,
    LINK-L2 H1..H6, C5-M4A V5 (C2 telemetry or live sample rows);
  marked PROXIES (reported, not in the selection): C3-L4-S1 H1..H4, LINK-L1 H1..H6
    (heartbeat proxies);
  also reported: C5-M5 S2 (C2 telemetry) and C5-M5 S3's 7000 stretch (C2 telemetry from the
    session start to the real entry at 06:57:33Z; the reports after it are 1080p and are not used
    here); C5-M4's 5-min b holds beside.
Per hold and candidate: entries; minutes from the hold's first report to the first entry; on
the same hold's own later reports (720p data -- a preview, not a 1080p measurement), the fraction
of the fresh reports after the first entry that are NOT clean by the blend; the best window.
ENTRY SELECTION RULE: the STRICTEST candidate (order above) whose first entry comes within 20
minutes (<= 1200 s from the hold's first report) on AT LEAST 7 OF THE 10 real-input holds. If none
does, the loosest candidate (E405) is reported as not enough and the entry stays at 435 of 450
(the user decides).

SECTION 2 -- THE LEAVE: CANDIDATES, REPLAY, SELECTION
Candidates, RUNG-ONLY (they are evaluated only while the level is 12600; never at a 720p level),
over the evaluated reports at the rung since the last SSRC change and its blackout (a window
cleared at every SSRC change, as the blend's; stale, blackout and resync reports do not enter it;
a missing value never counts toward a bar):
  L-A  lost_packets_delta >= 100 on >= 2 of the last 10 evaluated reports;
  L-B  the sum of lost_packets_delta over the last 15 evaluated reports >= 300 (~30 s; the
       window must hold 15 reports);
  L-C  lost_packets_delta >= 50 on >= 3 of the last 10 (the mild bar's loss half, no fps half);
  L-D  L-A, or stale_output_drops_delta >= 5 in the newest evaluated report (the client's
       decoder falling behind). On heartbeat-proxy series there is no per-report stale field, so
       L-D reads as L-A there (stated in the table).
Each, when met and no existing trigger has named a class for the report (the strict capacity /
queue-gap FALLBACK, the mild bar and the queue-gap ROUTINE keep their precedence and mapping
unchanged), names class ROUTINE, trigger/reason rung_loss, target one rung down = 7000 at
1280x720, through every existing gate: ROUTINE's hold-downs (60 reports after an up), the
blackout, guards, rate limit, the oscillation guard, the 10-min re-entry hold, the rung
oscillation HOLD. The existing mild bar, strict capacity and the backstop stay.
Replay (the policy starts AT the rung as if it had just entered: last direction up, 0 reports
since, the 3-report blackout; the series' elapsed offset by 60 s for the session-age guard, as
C5-M5 ran it) on:
  the 13 recorded 1080p series: C5-M1 A1 A2 A3; C5-M2 n1 C1 C2 C3; C5-M2 n1r C1 C2 C3;
    C5-M4 c_1x c_2x c_4x c_8x;
  C5-M5 S1's 1080p stretch (C2 telemetry from the entry's transition_done, 23:45:40.569Z, to the
    injected leave, 23:47:59.489Z; ~139 s);
  C5-M5 S3's 1080p stretch (C2 telemetry from the entry's transition_done, 06:57:34.630Z, to the
    session's end; ~8.5 min, loss 2.0/min). A leave there is a FALSE LEAVE.
Reported per series and candidate: seconds from the stretch's start to the leave and its trigger;
t_permit = the time of the first report at which the blackout and ROUTINE's post-up hold-down
allow a decrease; seconds from t_permit to the leave.
THE CLOCK OF THE BOUNDS (an interpretation, fixed here before data): every candidate inherits the
same post-entry hold-down (60 reports + the 3-report blackout, ~126 s), which by itself exceeds
both of the task's bounds when they are read from the stretch's start, so read that way no
candidate could meet them by construction. The bounds are therefore read from t_permit: "leaves
within X s" = (leave time - t_permit) <= X. Both readings are tabled.
LEAVE SELECTION RULE: the candidate that (1) leaves S1's stretch within 60 s, (2) leaves C5-M2
n1r C3 within 120 s, (3) does NOT leave on S3's stretch, and among those fires (leaves at all) on
the most of the 13 series; ties -> the simplest, L-A before L-B before L-C before L-D. If none
meets all three hard conditions, the table is reported and the mild bar is kept (the user
decides).
The selection (both rules) is written into the record WITH THE ROWS THAT CHOSE IT before any
companion code changes.

SECTION 3 -- THE BUILD, TESTS, MUTATIONS, THE STOP RULE

  PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_TOP=1080p. A test-only injection class RUNG_LOSS (INJECT=1, loopback,

  Any difference -> no session runs; the record says why.

SECTION 5 -- THE LOOK: LEVERS, COST TABLE, PRESETS
Mechanism (one, chosen here): with PRIVYHUB_PS1_LOOK set to a known preset, the companion, at a
PS1 (Beetle PSX HW) launch, writes a session core-options file in its runtime directory -- the
adopted Beetle PSX HW.opt read (never written) with the preset's keys replaced -- and points that
launch at it through the regenerated privyhub-session.cfg (global_core_options + core_options_path);
the file is removed at the session's end (BACK / session_ended) and at the next launch. Nothing
in ~/.config/retroarch is written. A title with its own per-title .opt (the six) keeps RetroArch's
precedence (its own file wins), so the look applies to titles without one (the attract title
Tekken 3 has none); stated in TOOLS.md. Unset -> the session cfg is byte-identical to today's and
nothing else changes. A smoke (one launch) must show RetroArch loading the session file before
any cost hold; if it does not, the mechanism is recorded as not working and the cost holds use
C5-M4's per-hold game options file (Tekken 3 (USA).opt in the core's config folder, created and
deleted per hold) instead, and the presets wait.
The adopted .opt (base + six) and Beetle PSX HW.cfg are hashed before and after EVERY session of
this task; any change is a failure of the row "adopted files byte-identical".
Levers (only the values this core build offers; MSAA is described by the core as Vulkan-only and
the core runs OpenGL here, so it is listed as NOT OFFERED unless the smoke shows otherwise; a
filter value RetroArch rewrites or rejects is NOT OFFERED):
  base_4x       the adopted config;
  dither_off    beetle_psx_hw_dither_mode = "disabled";
  filter_<v>    beetle_psx_hw_filter = each of "xBR", "SABR", "bilinear", "3-point", "JINC2";
  pgxp          beetle_psx_hw_pgxp_mode = "memory only", _pgxp_texture = "enabled",
                _pgxp_vertex = "enabled";
  msaa_4x       beetle_psx_hw_msaa = "4x" (only if offered);
  full          4x + the best-cost filter + dither_off + pgxp.
THE BEST-COST FILTER: among the filter_<v> holds that HOLD 60, the lowest GPU busy mean (ties:
lower GPU busy p95, then the listed order). None holds -> the full preset is 4x + dither_off +
pgxp (filter nearest), stated.
Cost holds: C5-M4's method and bar, 5 min each, Tekken 3 attract with zero input, the adopted
7000 stream, live by default, the counter-only game override (C5-M4A's c5_m4a_counter.sh) for
RetroArch's frame counter, T2 at 10 s, c5_m4_sampler at 1 s, C2 telemetry. A lever HOLDS 60 if:
  R1 RetroArch's counter >= 59.88 fps over the hold and no 256-frame interval > 4,278.7 ms;
  R2 the encoder's progress fps median >= 59.5 and dup+drop growth <= 0.5 % of frames.
  (C5-M4's R3 -- codec_ms -- is not evaluable: the C2 telemetry carries none; not a row.)
Reported per hold: HOLDS 60; GPU busy mean / p95; GPU W; RetroArch and encoder % of a core (T2);
Tctl max; the stream's per-second largest frame p50 / p90 and cap hits s/min; the client's fps,
stale and loss (not judged).
Then THE FULL PRESET AT THE RUNG once: 5 min, TOP=1080p and INJECT=1, INCREASE_1080P injected
after >= 90 s at PLAYING, the same rows over the 1080p minutes. Reported.
PRESETS behind PRIVYHUB_PS1_LOOK=: "4x" (today's adopted config; the same as unset) and
"remaster" (the full preset, its exact option values named in the record) -- offered ONLY if the
full preset HOLDS 60 in its 720p hold. If it misses, "remaster" is NOT offered (the flag accepts
"4x" only) and no substitute preset is chosen after the data. The helper's "remaster-1080p" is
offered only if the full preset also holds 60 at the rung. Unset -> nothing changes anywhere.
tools/ps1_look.sh 4x | remaster | remaster-1080p: fake-run tested (a stubbed systemctl / curl /
adb path that records every call and checks the order: set flags, restart, wait PLAYING, for
-1080p inject the entry and see 1920x1080 in the status, print what to look at, wait for Enter,
end the session, unset every flag, restart, verify the adopted state). The real run is the user's;
nothing perceptual is a gate.
