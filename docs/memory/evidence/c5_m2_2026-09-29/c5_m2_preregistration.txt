NIGHT 1 -- screening. Seven 20-minute attract-mode holds B1 C1 B2 C2 B3 C3 B4, adopted between every arm,
companion restarted through its unit before each with the selector set or unset, T2 on, any_override at
PLAYING, the first hold >= 40 min after the last session_ended, one thing on the host at a time. The
adaptive flag and PRIVYHUB_FEC_SCHEME unset.
  An arm PASSES SCREENING if its hold meets: spikes >= 20 ms < 200/min, post-FEC video loss < 10/min, max
  output gap <= 250 ms, rendered fps >= 59.5, stale drops < 20/min.
  Among passing arms the CONFIRMATION CANDIDATE is the one with the lowest post-FEC loss; ties broken by the
  higher bitrate.
  If no arm passes: NOT CAPABLE (all arms); night 2 is NOT RUN; each arm's rows and burst figures recorded.
  The four B holds must meet the baseline (the same five rows); if two or more miss, the night is
  INCONCLUSIVE (link) and is re-run once before anything else is decided.
  Reported for every 1080p hold, not gated: cap hits per minute (seconds whose largest frame is >= 95 % of
  the cap), the per-second largest frame p50/p90/max (the IDR-size proxy), frames of >= 80 packets per
  minute, on-air Mbit/s, host CPU / GPU load, T2 thermal, decoder queue depth, codec_ms.

NIGHT 2 -- confirmation. Six 20-minute holds B1 A1 B2 A2 B3 A3 with the confirmation candidate as A, exactly
as C5-M1 ran and scored: CAPABLE if every close-out row meets its target on all three A holds and the B holds
meet the baseline; CAPABLE WITH COST if all A holds meet the targets but a cost row is worse than noise by the
S1 rule (all three A worse than the worst B and the A median beyond it by more than the B spread), named; NOT
CAPABLE if any A hold misses any row, named. The first hold >= 40 min after night 1's last session_ended.
