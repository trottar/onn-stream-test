### Why the controller never stepped down

The shadow-derived triggers need either queued frames or long gaps:

- ROUTINE: queue ≥ 1 on ≥ 3/5;
- FALLBACK: fps < 50 on all 5 **and** (queue ≥ 2 on ≥ 3/5 **or** gap >
  250 on ≥ 2/5).

A capacity shortfall on this client:

- **drops** frames rather than queueing them (queue 0-1);
- **freezes** once the loss takes out enough of a GOP. A freeze longer
  than ~1.1 s is recovery's (`client_output_silence`, `DESYNC_MS` 1000).

So the only reports that met FALLBACK were inside those freezes. Each was
refused because `recovery_playing` was false (recovery already
PAUSED_RECOVERING; `game_not_paused` false too):

## What this means for the controller

- **Loss alone (F2) was correctly ignored.**
- **The kill switch works under a fault.**
- **The recovery interlock works.** Every FALLBACK inside a freeze was
  refused, and recovery owned the freeze.
- **The gap is the trigger, not the machinery.** A capacity shortfall
  shows up as low fps together with heavy post-FEC loss, not as queued
  frames. The two rules the user approved on 2026-09-29 answer exactly
  that:
  - the capacity trigger;
  - the backstop, for when recovery keeps restarting at the same level.
