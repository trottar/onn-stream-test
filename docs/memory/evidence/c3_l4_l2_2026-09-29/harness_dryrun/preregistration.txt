C3-L4-L2 -- pre-registration for the user's nft night (C3.L4 under real loss)
Written 2026-09-29 by Code, before the night, from the L1 record's proposed rule (section 7) with the
blend's expectations. The user may amend this file before starting; tools/c3_l4_nft_night.py prints it
at start and records its sha256 in the run directory. Rules are not tightened or loosened after data.

Controller as built for the night: PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_MODE=live (never INJECT); FALLBACK -> 5000,
ROUTINE -> 6000, one transition per event; INCREASE one rung when >= 85 of the last 90 reports since the
last SSRC change (and its 3-report blackout) are clean (fps >= 57, queue <= 1, gap <= 150); hold-downs
FALLBACK 30/60, ROUTINE 60, INCREASE 60/30 reports (after a down / after an up); oscillation guard (third
direction change in 10 min -> HOLD for the session); rate limit 4 per 10 min; guards incl. recovery
PLAYING and session age >= 60 s. Faults: host output hook, video port 48100 only (F3 adds audio 48101).

Classification: WORKS UNDER LOSS if F1, F2, F3 and K all hold as below; otherwise each row is recorded
as met / not met, with the per-report sample rows saying why.

F1 -- capacity cap (CAP = the midpoint of the calibrated 5500 and 6000 wire rates), 12 min on, 5 min off:
  F1a exactly one decrease per event, to the mapped target: the first is FALLBACK 7000 -> 5000 (or ROUTINE
      7000 -> 6000 followed, if 6000 still fails, by FALLBACK 6000 -> 5000 >= 30 reports later);
  F1b the blend's climb under the cap: INCREASE 5000 -> 5500, then INCREASE 5500 -> 6000, each >= 93
      reports after the previous SSRC change; 6000 exceeds the cap -> FALLBACK 6000 -> 5000 no sooner
      than the 60-report reversal hold-down; then the next increase (the third direction change inside
      10 min) becomes HOLD (reason oscillation) and the stream stays at 5000 for the rest of the session.
      If the calibrated cap lets 6000 through clean, F1b reads "climbed to <rung> and stayed" and the
      HOLD row is NOT APPLICABLE (the cap is recorded; not a failure of the controller);
  F1c rate_limited never true; no two transitions closer than the hold-down table; never a ramp (no two
      transitions < 93 reports apart in the same direction);
  F1d after removal: nothing if HOLD; otherwise one rung per >= 93 reports; BACK resets to 7000.
F2 -- 2 % random loss on the video port, 10 min on, 5 min off:
  F2a at most one decrease over the 10 minutes, and no ramp; loss alone never a decrease (a decrease, if
      any, carries fps/queue/gap evidence in its row).
F3 -- the cap until the stream is at 5000, then a 15 s full drop of 48100 and 48101, then removed:
  F3a no controller transition while recovery is not PLAYING (refused/guard recovery_playing, or no
      report at all);
  F3b recovery's restart is at the held level (5000; C3-F1), then resumed; ssrc_change source
      recovery_restart; 3-report blackout; no level_sync to 7000.
K -- the kill switch during the cap:
  Ka disable #1 -> already_disabled false, #2 -> true; status mode shadow, configured_mode live, acts false;
  Kb after disabling, no acted transition (would_act rows only) and the stream stays at its level;
  Kc after BACK the next session reads mode live again.
All sessions: any_override false at PLAYING; decoder report stored; lifecycle clean except F3's intended
recovery; at the end no privyhub_fault table, the flag unset and absent from the manager and the environ,
stream 7000, no game active.
