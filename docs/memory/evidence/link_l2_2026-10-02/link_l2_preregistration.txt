CLASSIFICATION OF THE DAY (a hold "meets" if loss/min < 10)
  STILL MET    >= 5 of 6 holds meet.
  TIME OF DAY  the meets and the misses separate cleanly by clock: every miss inside one
               contiguous <= 12-hour window of the local clock (circular, midnight wraps), every
               meet outside it, with >= 2 meets and >= 2 misses.
  MOVED        <= 1 of 6 meets.
  MIXED        anything else; reported with the table, no conclusion drawn.
  Precedence as listed (STILL MET first). If fewer than 6 holds score: the same rules over the
  scored holds, except that STILL MET needs >= 5 meets and MOVED needs >= 5 scored holds with
  <= 1 meet; with fewer than 5 scored holds and no rule met the day is INCOMPLETE.
  For TIME OF DAY the "window" is judged on each hold's PLAYING time in local hours; the window
  is the shortest arc containing all misses and must contain no meet.
