## Two checks

**(i) The harness read `level 5000` after F3's BACK; after F1's it read
7000.** It is **a stale status field, not a reset defect.**

- The policy reset at BACK: `session_ended_reset` 18:09:16.4, before
  {1 transition, 5000} → level 7000.
- The status polls after BACK read stream `bitrate_kbps` 7000, state
  REFERENCE, 0 transitions — and `level` 5000.
- The status `level` is the wrapper's cached last stream bitrate
  (`_stream_kbps`). `session_ended()` did not clear it, so it held until
  the next report.
- F1 read 7000 only because its stream had ended at 7000. The teardown
  read 7000.
- It is fixed in `C3-L4-N2`: `session_ended()` now clears the cache.

**(ii) The `session_ended_reset` rows** (8 in the night, 4 at the end):
harmless duplicates.

- The games plugin resets the controller on `native-stream-stop` (BACK),
  on `games/stop` (the harness's and the teardown's), and at the next
  session's gameplay release.
- `end_session()` is idempotent. 6 of the 8 rows show "before: 0
  transitions at 7000".
