QUESTION
On the adopted profile with adaptive off, does post-FEC video loss < 10/min still hold, and if
not, does it depend on the time of day?

THE HOLDS
- Six 20-minute (1200 s) attract-mode holds of the PS1 reference title, zero input, BACK to end.
- Adopted profile native_game_720p60_reference at 7000 kbps; adaptive_bitrate mode off;
  any_override false; no PRIVYHUB_* in the user manager or the companion's environ
  (PRIVYHUB_FEC_SCHEME and the profile selector absent); APK de072762...835e checked at each end
  (no reinstall).
- Harness: CL-B1's copy of C5's (cl_b1_night.sh + c5_m2_run.sh + t2_sample.py from
  evidence/cl_b1_apk_2026-09-30/), copied byte-identical into this directory, one hold per
  invocation, EXPECT_SHA = the adopted APK, COLD_MIN = 30 (>= 30 min idle after the last
  session_ended before each hold). T2 sampler on (10 s) during each invocation. Teardown after
  each: no flags, stream 7000, no game, 0 NOW PLAYING banners.
- Schedule (host local time, America/New_York, EDT = UTC-4), one hold per local 4-hour block,
  4 hours apart, each launched at about :40 so the hold lies inside its block:
    H1 2026-09-30 12:40 (block 12-16)   H2 2026-09-30 16:40 (block 16-20)
    H3 2026-09-30 20:40 (block 20-24)   H4 2026-10-01 00:40 (block 00-04)
    H5 2026-10-01 04:40 (block 04-08)   H6 2026-10-01 08:40 (block 08-12)
  Each hold's UTC start (PLAYING) and the host's local hour (timedatectl) are recorded.
- A hold that fails to reach PLAYING (or fails its preflight) is retried at most twice
  (the two-retry limit), 5 min apart, inside its own block. A block whose hold cannot run is
  recorded NOT RUN.

SCORING (per hold, the D-BASE close-out table's rows and targets)
  spikes >= 20 ms / min          < 200        (decoder.spike_20_ms / minutes)
  rendered fps                   >= 59.5      (rendered_frames / duration, not recent_fps)
  stale output drops / min       < 20
  video loss / min, post-FEC     < 10         <-- THE CLASSIFYING ROW
  audio underruns / min          < 5
  max output gap ms              <= 100       <-- the row in question beside loss; reported
Only the loss row enters the classification. The max output gap and the other rows are reported
against their targets.

CLASSIFICATION OF THE DAY (a hold "meets" if loss/min < 10)
  STILL MET    >= 5 of 6 holds meet.
  TIME OF DAY  the meets and the misses separate cleanly by clock: every miss inside one
               contiguous <= 12-hour window of the local clock (circular, midnight wraps), every
               meet outside it, with >= 2 meets and >= 2 misses.
  MOVED        <= 1 of 6 meets.
  MIXED        anything else; reported with the table, no conclusion drawn.
  Precedence as listed (STILL MET first). If fewer than 6 holds score: the same rules over the
  scored holds, except that STILL MET needs >= 5 meets and MOVED needs >= 5 scored holds with
  <= 1 meet; with fewer than 5 scored holds and no rule met the day is INCOMPLETE.
  For TIME OF DAY the "window" is judged on each hold's PLAYING time in local hours; the window
  is the shortest arc containing all misses and must contain no meet.

THE HISTORY TABLE (A1)
Every >= 15-minute hold on the adopted profile with adaptive off or silent since D-BASE closed,
from records already on disk, tabled by date, UTC start, local hour, minutes, loss/min, max gap,
spikes, fps and (where T2 exists) onn link rate and RSSI, the Opal's signal, tx retries per hold
and channel utilization (where sampled). Bucketed by local 4-hour block and by date.
DESCRIPTIVE ONLY; it does not enter the classification.
