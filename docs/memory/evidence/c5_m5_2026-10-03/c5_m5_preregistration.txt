SECTION 1 -- FIRST AND GATING: THE MID-SESSION SIZE CHANGE (session R0)
How: the minimum test-only path. The actuator gains a level that carries a size (12,600 kbps at
1920x1080: the c3 arm's argv exactly, golden-checked); the live controller's test-only inject
route gains `class=SIZE&size=1080p|720p` (loopback, PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_INJECT=1, live, not
disabled), which requests one transition straight through the controller's own actuator worker
(serial lock, recovery interlock) with no policy rule. No rung, no policy change.
Session R0: one attract session (Tekken 3, zero input), live by default, INJECT=1 for this session
only, T2 at 10 s, the C2 telemetry (c5_m4_sampler --telemetry), SurfaceFlinger captured at 5 s
intervals. After >= 90 s at PLAYING: inject SIZE 1080p; 60 s later inject SIZE 720p; then 60 s more;
BACK.
  R0a  For EACH direction (7000/1280x720 -> 12,600/1920x1080, then back):
       - the actuator reports ok; the encoder argv after the switch carries scale/pad at the new
         size and -b:v / -maxrate at the new rate (encoder_command in native-stream-status);
       - the client reports exactly one SSRC change for the switch (report ssrc_changes total = 2);
       - the client decodes the new size: SurfaceFlinger's stream SurfaceView buffers read
         1920x1080 in a capture taken 5-55 s after the up switch and 1280x720 in one taken 5-55 s
         after the down switch;
       - rendering continues: rendered fps over each 60-s segment after a switch >= 55 (from the
         C2 telemetry's rendered_frames_delta);
       - no recovery cycle (no recovery-log event other than session_started / session_ended in
         the session) and no session restart (one decoder report for the session at BACK).
  R0b  Each direction's cost:
       - the output gap at the switch (the largest output_gap_ms among the report's retained
         slow events in [ssrc, ssrc + 1 s], LINK-L1's analysis method) <= 450 ms
         (2x the C3.L3a-S1 restart max 225);
       - sequence resyncs 0 outside the SSRC changes (report sequence_resyncs 0 and no
         sequence_resync discontinuity);
       - no stale-drop burst beyond the first second: stale_output_drops_delta 0 in every C2
         telemetry report that starts more than 1 s after the switch, within 10 s of it;
       - codec_ms at the switch (the same slow-event rows) and actuation_ms: reported.
  R0 PASSES if R0a and R0b hold for both directions. A failing session may be re-run at most
  twice (the two-retry limit) only for a harness/launch failure, never for a row that failed.
  IF R0a FAILS: STOP AFTER SECTION 1. Record what the client did (the decoder report, the C2
  telemetry, SurfaceFlinger, logcat lines from the codec, redacted) and list the client change the
  rung needs; sections 2-4 wait for the user.
  IF R0a holds and only R0b fails: also stop after section 1 and record the cost (the rung would
  carry a switch cost above the bound); sections 2-4 wait for the user.

SECTION 2 -- THE RUNG'S CONSTANTS (fixed here, before any replay)
- Level `1080p_12600`: 1920x1080, 12,600 kbps (-maxrate and -bufsize 12,600k), cap 90,000 B,
  GOP 15, bf 0, 8+1 FEC, the adopted cushion and redundancy. Every 720p level is 1280x720.
- Flag PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_TOP=1080p (exact, case-insensitive after strip); anything else or
  absent = no rung. Ladder with the flag: 5000 / 5500 / 6000 / 7000 / 12600(1080p).
- ENTRY: at 7000 only, class INCREASE, reason increase_1080p, when the rung window (the last
  N = 450 evaluated reports since the last SSRC change and its blackout; resyncs enter it as not
  clean, stale reports are skipped -- exactly the blend's window rules, only longer) is full and
  >= 435 of its 450 are clean (the blend's clean: fps >= 57, queue <= 1, gap <= 150), and every
  gate passes (the INCREASE hold-down as now: 30 reports after an up, 60 after a down; guards;
  rate limit; the oscillation guard; the re-entry hold-down below).
  N = 450 (15 min at the 2-s report rate), the middle of the allowed 300-600: five times the
  increase window, long enough that the rung is entered only after a sustained clean stretch, and
  short enough that a 30-min hold can show an entry and a 2-h night more than one opportunity
  after the 10-min re-entry hold-down.
- LEAVE: at 12600, the existing triggers with the existing mapping: the mild bar (capacity_mild:
  fps < 57 on >= 4/5 and lost >= 50 on >= 3/5) -> ROUTINE one rung down = 7000 at 1280x720; the
  strict capacity / the queue-gap FALLBACK -> 5000; the queue-gap ROUTINE -> 6000 (its fixed
  target, as now); the recovery-escalation backstop as now. Every existing hold-down applies as
  now (after the entry -- an up -- a decrease waits 60 reports).
- RE-ENTRY HOLD-DOWN: no entry for 10 minutes (600,000 ms on the policy clock) after any transition
  that leaves 12600 (refusal reason rung_reentry_hold).
- RUNG OSCILLATION: a leave, then an entry, then a leave in one session -> the second leave is
  carried out and the policy goes to HOLD (reason oscillation_rung) for the rest of the session
  at the level it left to (7000 on the mild bar): no further decision is acted on.
- Status: native-stream-status width / height = the active level's; the controller's status
  carries the level's size and the rung's window / hold-down.
- Recovery: a link-drop restart at 12600 restarts at 12600 / 1920x1080 (level-preserving); a full
  start resets to 7000 / 1280x720 (C1); the backstop still falls back to 5000.
- Test-only injection (INJECT=1): class INCREASE_1080P (the policy's own entry decision with the
  window requirement replaced by the injection, every other gate applies), class CAPACITY_MILD (a
  synthetic mild-bar decision through every gate), and section 1's raw SIZE.
- NOT adopted. The flag is off by default and never set outside a session here.
