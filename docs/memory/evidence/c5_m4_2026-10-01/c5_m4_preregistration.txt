THE ARMS (each 5 minutes, attract title Tekken 3, Beetle PSX HW, zero input)
  window 1920x1080 (RetroArch fullscreen on the 1920x1080 headless display; the 4:3 picture
  pillarboxed inside); internal resolution 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x.
  3x is run only if the core accepts it (its log line "Building OpenGL state (3x internal res.");
  otherwise it is reported as not offered by this core.
  8x only if 4x HOLDS 60 in (b) with >= 40 % headroom, defined as: GPU busy p95 <= 60 % AND the
  busiest logical CPU's p95 <= 60 % over the (b) hold.
  (a) no stream; (b) the adopted 7000 stream (live adaptive default, as it is); (c) the c3 arm
  native_game_1080p60_c3_80pct_cap90 behind PRIVYHUB_NATIVE_PROFILE_ID, for that hold only.

THE BAR (on the frame rate only; CPU/GPU/temperatures are reported, not judged)
  A scale HOLDS 60 in a hold if ALL of:
  R1  RetroArch drops no frames over the 5 minutes. Measured from RetroArch's own frame counter
      (the window title's "Frames:" field, which RetroArch rewrites every 256 frames; each rewrite
      timestamped as it happens with `xprop -spy`). Over the hold:
        frames-per-second from the counter >= 59.88 (60 Hz display; the 0.2 % allowance is the
        timestamping resolution, stated, not a tolerance for drops), and
        no 256-frame interval longer than 256/60 s + 12 ms (a missed vsync adds 16.7 ms).
      If RetroArch cannot be made to report the counter without drawing on the picture, R1 falls
      back to the title FPS field only and the record says so.
  R2  (b), (c) only: the capture delivers 60 fps to the encoder -- the encoder's own progress
      fps (ffmpeg stats in the companion log) >= 59.5 median over the hold, dup/drop growth
      <= 0.5 % of frames; and
  R3  (b), (c) only: no codec_ms growth -- the client's decode time (C2 telemetry, codec_ms) in
      the last minute's median is no more than 2 ms above the first minute's, and its max is
      reported.
  (a) is judged on R1 alone.

THE CANDIDATE
  The highest scale that HOLDS 60 in (b). Ties are impossible (one value per scale).
  If no scale above 1x holds in (b), the candidate is 1x at 1920x1080 and the record says so.

NOT GATES
  (c) is reported, not judged (one 5-minute hold is not a screening night).
  The client rows (fps, spikes, stale, loss, max gap) are reported for (b) and (c), not judged.
  The offline SSIM table is reported; nothing perceptual is a gate.

NOTHING ADOPTED. Teardown after each hold: selector unset and absent, stream 7000, no game.
