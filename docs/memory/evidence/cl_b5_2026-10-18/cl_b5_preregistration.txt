1. Client change (the debug APK). The Opus decode leaves the receive thread:
   - The receive thread keeps validation, de-dup, the arrival gap and OpusReorder's in-order release. What it
     releases (a frame, a given-up frame, an outage's concealment) is offered to a bounded queue (64 items, about
     640 ms of 10-ms frames) without blocking. If the queue is full, the item is dropped and counted; the socket read
     never waits on the codec.
   - A dedicated decode thread owns the MediaCodec (created on its first item, released at its end). It decodes in
     queue order and is the PCM queue's producer for an Opus session: the split packets, the concealment slots and
     the crossfade flag.

   - The session report's opus object gains decode_queue_depth_max, decode_queue_wait_max_us (enqueue to taken),
     decode_max_us (the codec call, kept) and decode_queue_drops.

     touches only receiveOpus, the Opus state, the snapshot's Opus fields and the end-of-loop release. The existing
     JVM tests are unchanged and green.
