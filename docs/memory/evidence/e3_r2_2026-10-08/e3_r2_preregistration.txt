THE ONE CHANGE, three parts, all behind PRIVYHUB_RECOVERY_TRIGGER=v2 (absent = today, byte for byte in behaviour):
  1. the gap log (also alone as PRIVYHUB_RECOVERY_GAP_LOG=1): the controller receive loop notes every client->host
     silence > 250 ms and, 500 ms after it ends, hands one line to its own writer thread (start, end, length, the
     packets of the catch-up burst in the 500 ms from the first returning packet, a truncated mark if a second gap
     opened inside them, the time written) -> logs/games/native_stream_ctrl_gaps.log, 512 KB + one rotated copy;
  2. the threshold: E3-R1's v2 as written (5,000 ms, confirmed on two checks >= 250 ms apart with no packet between);
  3. the exit: a pause whose trigger was controller_silence resumes by itself when (a) the controller age the
     recovery monitor reads is < 500 ms on two checks >= 250 ms apart and (b) a heartbeat received after (a) held
     shows last_output_age_ms in 0..300 (a stale one waits for the next). 300 ms: the task's figure; R1's
     idle_stats.json has the client's idle output age max at 144 ms (295 ms is the controller age max), so 300
     covers the idle maximum with margin and stays far below a real drop's ages (>= 1,000, rising 2 s per heartbeat).
     The resume is EmulatorManager.resume() -- the call the client's gate (native-stream-ready) makes -- and the
     state moves to PLAYING with a `resumed` line (via controller_return, the ages, packets-back-to-resume ms).
     Nothing is loaded (R3c2 untouched); it needs the encoder up; a failed resume stays paused, logs
     controller_exit_failed and waits for a newer heartbeat; GIVE_UP_MS (120 s) still applies; a
     client_output_silence pause keeps its own exit.
