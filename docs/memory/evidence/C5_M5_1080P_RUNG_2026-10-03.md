## 2. The rung, as built (the patch record has the detail)

- **The level `1080p_12600`**: 1920×1080 at 12,600 kbps. Its argv equals
  C5-M2's c3-arm golden exactly. Every 720p level is 1280×720, and its
  argv is byte-identical to `golden_after_unset`.
- **The flag `PRIVYHUB_ADAPTIVE_BITRATE_TOP=1080p`.** The ladder becomes
  5000 / 5500 / 6000 / 7000 / 12600; absent, it is the closed
  controller's.
- **Entry** `increase_1080p`: from 7000 only, when ≥ 435 of the last 450
  reports since the last SSRC change are clean (the blend's clean), and
  every gate passes.
- **Leave**: the existing triggers. The mild bar goes to 7000 / 720p;
  strict and the queue/gap FALLBACK go to 5000; the queue/gap ROUTINE goes
  to 6000; the backstop is as before.
- **Re-entry hold**: 10 min after a leave. **Rung oscillation**: leave,
  entry, leave → HOLD for the session.
- **Actuator**: `NativeStreamManager.adaptive_level_transition`. Between
  720p levels it is the C3.L3a transition, unchanged. To or from the rung
  it is the same encoder-only restart, with the scale/pad at the level's
  size.
  - Recovery at the rung restarts at 1920×1080. The loopback c3 route
    cannot reach the rung.
  - `native-stream-status` `width` / `height` follow the level, and the
    guards are untouched (`any_override` stays false).
- **Changed:** 4 companion files (`companion_patch.diff`).
- **Unchanged:**
  - the shadow, byte-identical (`d66211b3…`);
  - the drop-in, the profile, the APK and the client.

## 5. What it means — for the user

- **The rung works mechanically.** The size change needs no client
  change. The entry and the leave act through the same actuator. Every
  sized switch costs one gap of 287-403 ms (against 128-225 ms for a
  bitrate-only restart). Recovery and the guards hold at the rung.
- **On this link, the rung is rarely reached and loosely guarded.**
  - The 15-minute clean window was met once in about 2.4 h of live sessions
    at 7000 (S3, at 111 min), and on none of the ten recorded holds with
    real per-report inputs.
  - Once at 1080p, the leave rule waits for a sustained shortfall, which
    the 1080p's bursty loss does not produce. S1's 2.3 min ran 80 lost
    per minute without meeting it, while S3's 8.5 min ran 2.0.
  - So the rung holds 1080p through bursts that the 720p rows would call
    misses. Whether that is acceptable is a picture question, and **the
    user's look is the gate**.
- **Not adopted.** The flag is off by default and nowhere set. Adopting
  it, its `nft` capacity night, and any change to N or to the leave are
  the user's.
