## 2. The floors, final

> **Two caveats, both binding on every number in this section.**
> **CPU-time ≠ slow-core.** `CPUQuota=` is CFS bandwidth on this host's
> full-speed cores: a machine with fewer, equally fast cores behaves
> like this; a machine with slower cores does not, exactly.
> **cgroup ≠ host.** The floors are for the stack's cgroup (companion,
> RetroArch, the encoder, the grabber children). Xorg (~10 % of a core,
> needed for capture), pulseaudio, the desktop and the OS are outside it.
> No lower-tier hardware was measured; no lower-tier claim is made.
